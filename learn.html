Learn Mössbauer spectroscopy

Short guides for anyone new to the technique. Most have interactive plots, so you can see how a spectrum changes as you move a slider.

For a detailed guide to measuring soils and sediments, see our review, Current practices for analyzing soils and sediments via Mössbauer spectroscopy (Thompson and others, 2025, Journal of Plant Nutrition and Soil Science).
